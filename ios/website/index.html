01 · Leave alerts

“Leave in 10 minutes.”
Not “practice at 4.”

A calendar tells you when something starts. HeliPad tells you when to walk out the door — the start time, minus the drive, minus your buffer — and buzzes ten minutes before that.

  • Timed from the drive

    Every reminder is set from the leave-by time, so a longer trip means an earlier nudge.

  • Fires without signal

    Alerts are scheduled on the phone itself, so they still arrive in a car park with no bars.

  • Driver needed

    A stop with nobody assigned flags itself 12 hours ahead, while there's still time to sort it.

  • Gentle check-ins

    If a stop wraps up and nobody ticked it off, you get one quiet question — mark it done, or ask again in an hour.
